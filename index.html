01 / Artist Statement — Scale & Solitude

Vast Terrain & Quiet Presence.

My photographic work explores the contrast between monumental landscapes and the fleeting stillness of standing within them. Across glacial valleys, ridgelines, and open snowfields, a single human figure or distant peak anchors the immense scale of the natural world.

By stepping back to let the environment breathe, these frames invite the viewer to experience the quiet weight and solitude of high-altitude spaces.

02 / Artist Statement — Light & Time

Capturing Energy Across Time.

Beyond static landscapes, the camera serves as an instrument for recording time and invisible motion. Using extended shutter speeds in pitch-black industrial tunnels, showered sparks carve incandescent geometric arcs around a still silhouette, reflecting off wet concrete below.

Whether balancing high-dynamic-range twilight horizons or isolating warm chandelier glow in historic interiors, light is treated as a physical, sculptural material.

03 / Artist Statement — Perspective

Reframing the Familiar.

Just as important as where the camera points is what it chooses to frame out. By shooting through foreground foliage, cathedral arches, or steep canyon walls, familiar landmarks and sacred spaces take on an intimate, contemplative distance.

Together, this collection documents a journey of deliberate observation—slowing down to notice where color, geometry, and atmosphere converge.

03 / About the Photographer

Preston Breeze

SALT LAKE CITY, UTAH
IMG_0881 // ARTIST PORTRAIT

Exploring the Intersection of Technical Precision & Natural Wonder.

Based in Salt Lake City, Utah, Preston Breeze approaches photography with both an engineer’s eye for optics, light, and timing and an outdoorsman’s appreciation for wild alpine spaces.

Whether hiking into high-altitude basins, experimenting with long-exposure light painting underground, or isolating quiet architectural details at dusk, his work focuses on creating images that feel immersive, deliberate, and tactile.
