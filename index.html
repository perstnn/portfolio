01 / The Philosophy / Part I

The Pursuit.

For me, photography has never been about staging the perfect subject or obsessing over clinical perfection. It is a pursuit of the genuine vibe of a moment—translating exactly what it felt like to be there into a single frame.

01 / The Philosophy / Part II

The Environment.

Whether that happens under the neon wash of a late-night jam session, in the cold, thin air of an alpine ridge, or just walking through a quiet city street at dusk, the goal remains the same. I'm looking for the energy that exists right before the shutter clicks.

01 / The Philosophy / Part III

The Capture.

It comes down to patience, atmosphere, and an awareness of how light shapes a space. Every photograph in this collection is a piece of how I experience the world—a continuous attempt to see the sound, feel the scale, and catch the fleeting motion of life as it happens.

THE ARTIST CLICK TO VIEW BOTH PORTRAITS [+]

Chasing the Vibe Through Sound, Light, and Landscape.

I am a photographer, musician, and electrical engineering student based in Salt Lake City, Utah. Whether I have a guitar in my hands or a camera on my shoulder, I am always paying attention to tone, atmosphere, and the feeling of a space.

In my music photography, I try to capture sound visually by leaning into moody lighting, amplifier glow, and the raw energy of a room. When I head up into the mountains or travel, that same focus turns toward big alpine views, natural light, and candid moments with the people around me.
